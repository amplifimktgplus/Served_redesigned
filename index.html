Good to know

Questions, answered.

Fees, cancellations, payments and how many hours you can grab.

How do I book a court?

Find your court, pick an orange day, choose your time and court, then pay online. Nothing is reserved until payment, and the court is held while you pay, so nothing is double-booked.

How much does Served cost a player?

Searching and comparing courts costs nothing. You pay the venue’s rate plus a 5% service fee, shown before you pay.

Can I cancel a booking?

Cancel up to 24 hours before your slot and the court price is refunded in full. Only the service fee is kept.

How many hours can I book at once?

As many as you need, up to the whole day. Pick single hours, or tap Whole day to take every open hour on a court. The hours do not have to be next to each other, and you can book more than one court in the same booking.

How can I pay?

Online payments by GCash, Maya, QR Ph, bank and card.

What does it cost to list my court?

₱0 to list, every month. No subscription, no commission: you receive the full court price on every booking.
